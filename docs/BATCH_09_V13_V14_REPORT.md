# Batch 9 — V13 Developer Market + V14 Help, Trust & Knowledge

## Purpose

Batch 9 makes SecurePay easier to enter from two very different directions:

- a builder who has made an application and wants to connect SecurePay; and
- a trader or participant who wants a clear answer before continuing.

The governing laws are:

- **Developer Market:** start with what the builder is trying to make money do, then translate into SecurePay structure underneath.
- **Knowledge Market:** short answer first, example second, full reference when needed.

## V13 — Developer Market

The preview and authenticated developer journey now treat the developer area as a Market room rather than an API console.

The visual journey is:

1. What did you build?
2. What should money do?
3. How do you want to connect?
4. Try the supported path in sandbox.
5. SecurePay Integration Check verifies the connection facts.

The builder is not required to know SecureLink, Group SecureLink, SecureFlow or Group SecureFlow before starting. SecurePay maps the stated money shape to the underlying topology.

### AI handoff law

A short-lived SecureCode may guide an AI builder where supported. Permanent credentials remain in the application's secret manager. A coding agent cannot certify its own SecurePay integration.

### Authority boundary

The client may consume SecurePay truth and request actions. It does not calculate or invent Payment Ready, settlement, release authority, agreement authority, participant authority, fees or financial state.

Sandbox success is not production approval.

## V14 — Help, Trust & Knowledge

The Help front door is now intention-first:

- Ask in normal language.
- Start from a real-life situation.
- Browse a topic only when useful.

Knowledge uses progressive disclosure:

1. Short answer — what this means now.
2. Example — see it in a real situation.
3. Full reference — trust, security, compliance, terms or privacy when deeper detail is required.

Help does not become a substitute for legal, financial or professional advice and does not create financial truth.

## Formal knowledge rooms

Trust, Security, Compliance, Not a Bank and Legal now share a clearer knowledge navigation layer and the Living SecurePay Mark. The mark is polite by default and becomes more present only where guidance or caution requires it.

The Trust hero has been moved away from a heavy dark presentation toward a calmer clarity room, while preserving the product boundary that SecurePay is not a bank, insurer, guarantor, court, investment platform or legal representative.

## Responsive and accessibility

Batch 9 provides mobile collapse rules for the developer rail, connection choices, help search, knowledge maps and trust presentation. Primary interactive controls keep a 44px minimum target. Reduced-motion preferences disable decorative transition and animation behaviour in Batch 9 surfaces.

## Production truth

`/preview/developers` remains a DEV-only visual surface and issues no credentials. The canonical `/developers` journey keeps its existing backend registration, SecureCode, credential and Integration Check calls.

Help and formal knowledge pages remain public guidance surfaces. No backend financial authority has been moved into the browser.
