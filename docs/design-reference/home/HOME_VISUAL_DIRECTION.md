# SecurePay Home — Approved Visual Direction

Approved: 21 August 2026

## Product feeling

The Market leads. SecurePay supports.

The trader should feel they have arrived in their own market space. SecurePay is present as calm infrastructure: it listens, interprets the trader's intention, keeps the agreement clear, and guides the next step without dominating the page.

## Locked visual language

- Green-led SecurePay palette with restrained orange accents.
- Warm off-white background, charcoal text, generous whitespace.
- Official SecurePay logo only; never redraw or alter it.
- Large faint green SecurePay icon in the background.
- Very subtle green/orange travelling-light traces inspired by the approved opening animation.
- Lively icon-led Trade and Life & Support choices; no stock photography.
- Serif display type for the welcome; clean sans-serif for actions and agreement facts.
- Soft rounded cards, light borders and quiet shadows. Avoid glassmorphism and loud gradients.
- Desktop: trader intention on the left, SecurePay-understood demo on the right.
- Mobile: welcome + intention first, then SecurePay-understood demo, then Trade and Life & Support choices.
- Mobile must fit 360px Android and 390px iPhone without horizontal clipping.

## Interaction law

The trader speaks naturally. SecurePay responds clearly.

The Home never requires the trader to know SecureLink, Group SecureLink, SecureFlow or Group SecureFlow before describing the intended outcome. Product classification remains underneath the experience.

## Demo law

“SecurePay understood” is an illustrative interpretation, not a declaration of live backend or financial truth. It may demonstrate parties, purpose, amount, conditions and intended money movement, but must not claim that money is funded, Payment Ready, released or settled unless that fact comes from the backend in a live journey.

## Theme-ready foundation

The Home atmosphere is intentionally tokenized so future Market themes can vary decorative background, seasonal motifs and icon accents while preserving layout, meaning, status semantics and the official logo.

## Approved v4 interaction refinements — 21 Aug 2026

- Reduce the official header logo so the Market begins higher and Trade/Life cards remain visible above the fold on normal laptops.
- The first click/focus in the intention box clears the illustrative example and leaves a ready-to-type field.
- Typing updates the **SecurePay understood** demo; explicit KES/budget amounts may be reflected as illustrative interpretation, while unknown facts remain **To be confirmed**.
- The understood demo ends with **Use this information to continue**, preserving the intention into the canonical creation journey.
- The homepage provides only a subtle downward invitation to the deeper explanation. Traders who already understand SecurePay do not have to consume training material.
- Trade/Life and agreement icons should feel alive through brief, purposeful micro-motion (meet, move, grow, confirm, help). Motion must stop at rest and respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Live agreement and financial truth always comes from SecurePayAPI; the homepage demo remains explicitly illustrative.
