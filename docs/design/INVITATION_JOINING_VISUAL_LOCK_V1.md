# SecurePay V4 — Invitation & Joining Visual Lock v1

## Purpose
The invitation doorway must answer, before asking for action:
1. What trade is inviting me in?
2. What role am I being asked to take?
3. What money is involved?
4. What has happened already?
5. What has **not** happened yet?
6. What can I safely do next?

## Visual law
- An invitation is a doorway into an agreement, not evidence that an agreement is already accepted.
- Creator / proposer is not automatically the payer.
- Viewing is quiet and informative.
- Signing in establishes the acting KS identity but does not join.
- Joining is a recorded participation step, not confirmation.
- Confirmation is the participant's own confirmation of an exact version only.
- Expiry is a real boundary. The UI must not visually imply continued authority.
- Group invitation screens distinguish contribution intention from backend-confirmed money facts.

## Living SecurePay Mark
- guiding: reading / orientation;
- success: the local join step succeeded, with explicit language that no money state follows from that alone;
- complete: the participant's own confirmation step completed;
- caution + commanding: expired invitation, ownership mismatch, or another real blocker.

The official symbol never changes. Status attaches to it.

## Current backend boundary
The current public invitation preview does not expose the inviter identity. Canonical SecurePay therefore does not invent "who invited you". The V4 fixture may demonstrate the desired composition with an explicit fixture warning only.

## Mobile law
The invitation becomes one continuous story on small screens. Role, amount and purpose must be readable before the primary action. No horizontal page overflow is permitted.
