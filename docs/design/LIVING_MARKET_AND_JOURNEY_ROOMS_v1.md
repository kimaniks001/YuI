# SecurePay Living Market & Journey Rooms — v1

Status: implementation reference
Date: 21 August 2026

## Principle

The Market entrance creates curiosity. My Market creates momentum. Journey rooms create confidence.

SecurePay should feel alive without becoming noisy. Motion is used as information:

- **Needs attention:** the most alive. Small pulse, nudge, or story icon motion tells the trader where their next move matters.
- **Waiting:** deliberately calm. Nothing is wrong and nothing is required from the trader at that moment.
- **Completed:** settled and satisfying. A small completion gesture, then rest.
- **Journey questions:** supportive and affirming, never an exam room. The trader should feel that SecurePay is carrying their earlier answers forward.

## Journey-room language

Use warm, capable language such as:

- “Good start — we already have the thread.”
- “You’re doing well. Keep going one clear choice at a time.”
- “Almost there — the important parts are taking shape.”
- “Answering these questions does not move money. We are only shaping the agreement.”

Avoid language that sounds like grading, testing, scolding, or intimidating the trader.

## Visual treatment

- SecurePay green leads; orange is used for attention and confirmation accents.
- Very faint SecurePay geometry may live on the walls.
- Fine green/orange trails can suggest movement through the Market.
- Cards keep generous white space but should not float in empty beige rooms.
- Tiny story icons may wiggle, breathe, meet, grow, confirm or help.
- Motion should stop when the meaning has been communicated.
- Reduced-motion preferences must be respected.

## Doctrine boundary

The UI may guide, animate, display and request actions. It must not create financial truth. Payment state, Payment Ready, release, settlement, quorum, authority and balances remain backend facts.
