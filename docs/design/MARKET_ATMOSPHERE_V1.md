# SecurePay Market Atmosphere v1

> **SecurePay does not wear costumes. The Market has seasons.**

Themes change atmosphere around the trader, never the meaning of agreement, identity, money, review or status truth.

## Stable atmospheres
- **Market Day** — default warm-cream daylight; open, familiar, calm.
- **Green Market** — fresher leafy edges; growing and connected.
- **Town Market** — warm clay/stone edges; practical and grounded.
- **Evening Market** — quiet deep-green edge treatment; focused and assured.

## Theme boundary
A theme may change canvas atmosphere, faint washes, restrained travelling-light traces and decorative Market texture. It may not change the official mark, green/orange status semantics, agreement roles, Payment Ready/release/settlement meaning, fees, quotes, balances, rail eligibility, verification or authority.

## Opening ritual
The opening is a short orientation gesture: two participant points approach a common point, a path becomes visible, and the official SecurePay mark appears as the quiet guide. It is not a literal lighthouse, streetlamp or mascot.

Rules: once per browser session on real signed-out Home, always skippable, no financial claim, reduced-motion aware, and the approved Home remains unchanged after dismissal.

## User preference
Stable atmosphere may be selected locally in Settings. It is a device-side visual preference only and is separate from backend account, security, settlement and financial settings.
