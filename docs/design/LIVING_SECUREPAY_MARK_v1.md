# Living SecurePay Mark v1

## Design law
**The SecurePay mark is the light. We do not draw a lighthouse around it.**

The official green SecurePay symbol is never redrawn, recoloured, distorted or replaced. State is communicated by the space around it: halo, movement and a small companion badge.

### Presence
- **Polite** — normal headers, navigation and orientation.
- **Present** — listening and guidance.
- **Commanding** — rare confirmation or caution; command comes from context, not an oversized logo.

### States
`resting`, `listening`, `guiding`, `success`, `caution`, `waiting`, `review`, `complete`.

### Product safety
Financial success/completion/Payment Ready/release/settlement expressions must come from SecurePayAPI. Fixture/demo screens remain labelled as fixture/demo truth.

### Placement
1. Signed-out Market Home may use the approved full wordmark.
2. Internal Market, auth and journey headers use LivingSecurePayMark.
3. Footer may use the full wordmark as signature.
4. Decorative guide lights are low-opacity and hidden from assistive technology.
5. The mark must never become wallpaper.

Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Constitutional reference
This component is governed by `SECUREPAY_VISUAL_CONSTITUTION_v1.md`. Presence and state are semantic; status decoration must not be chosen solely for visual variety.
