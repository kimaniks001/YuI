# Help, Trust & Knowledge Visual Lock v1

**Law:** Clarity before density.

Ordinary help begins with a question or situation, not a documentation taxonomy. The information depth is progressive:

1. short answer;
2. example;
3. full reference.

Formal Trust, Security, Compliance and Legal rooms remain available and serious, but they are reference rooms rather than the everyday front door.

The Living SecurePay Mark stays polite in reference material and becomes more present only when guidance or caution is useful.
